Questions?
We've got you.

Clear answers about using SellFlow's free tools.

Are these tools really free?

Yes. The tools in this preview are free to use and don't require an account or payment.

Do I need to create an account?

No account is needed. Enter information directly into the tool you want to use.

Are my numbers uploaded anywhere?

In this version, calculations run in your browser. The tool doesn't send your entries to a SellFlow server or save them to an online account. If you close or refresh the page, most entries will be cleared.

Can I use the invoice for my business?

Yes. Add your business and customer details, enter the items, then choose Print / Save PDF. Review the invoice before sending it to your customer.

Does SellFlow guarantee business results?

No. These tools help with calculations and admin, but your results depend on your costs, customers and decisions. Check your inputs and seek professional advice where appropriate.
